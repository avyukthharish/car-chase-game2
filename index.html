How to Play

01

Start the Engine

Click anywhere inside the game area, then press the ↑ arrow key to accelerate. This also enables sound.

02

Switch Lanes

Use ← and → to move between the seven lanes. Your car shifts cleanly without skidding.

03

Brake When Needed

Press ↓ to slow down and avoid collisions. Use it wisely when traffic gets tight.

04

Survive & Score

Each car you pass adds points. The longer you survive, the faster the game gets — and the higher your score climbs.

05

Beat Your Best

Your highest score is saved automatically. Press R to restart and try to beat it.
